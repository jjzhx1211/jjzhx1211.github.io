About

Zhuoxuan Jiang is a Lecturer/Researcher and Master Supervisor at the Faculty of Business Information, Shanghai Business School. He received his Ph.D. degrees from Peking University in 2018. He was a visiting student at Nanyang Technological University from 2016 to 2017, worked in IBM Research-China (2018-2020), JD AI Research (2020-2021), Tencent Youtu Lab (2021-2022), Learnable.ai (a startup, 2022-2025) and joined the faculty of Shanghai Business School in 2024.

The group welcomes master and bachelor's students. If you are passionate about large language models, agent AI, embodied intelligence and their applications, please feel free to contact him by email.
